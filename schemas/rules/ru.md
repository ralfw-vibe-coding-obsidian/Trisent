# House rules: RU

Decisions that hold for **every** Russian text. Glosses and translations
are written in **{{yourLanguage}}**. This list grows.

## Base forms

1. Verbs carry the **infinitive**: `пишу` → `писать`, `иду` → `идти`.
2. **Aspect partners are two different words with two keys**:
   `писать` and `написать` do not share a key. They are learned as a
   pair - the description names the partner - but they are counted
   separately, because knowing one does not mean knowing the other.
3. Verbs of motion keep their pairs apart as well: `идти` and `ходить`
   are two words.
4. Nouns take the **nominative singular**: `столе` → `стол`, `книги` →
   `книга`. All cases of a noun share one key; the case is shown in the
   gloss, not in the key.
5. Adjectives take the **masculine nominative singular**: `большую` →
   `большой`. Short forms share the key with the full ones.
6. Names: base form as written (`Иван`, `Москва`), the gloss is the name.
7. Pronouns take the base form of their own series: `меня` → `я`,
   `него` → `он`. The `н-` after a preposition is not a separate word.

## Where Russian hides grammar

8. **Case lives in the ending, and the gloss must show it.** `в столе` →
   "in table(prep)", `на стол` → "onto table(acc)". Without that, the
   whole point of the layer is lost: the reader cannot see why the same
   word looks different.
9. **The particle is a word**: `ли`, `бы`, `же`, `не` stand on their own.
   The suffixed `-то` and `-нибудь` stay with their word.
10. Reflexive `-ся` stays **part of the verb**: `учиться` is one word
    with its own key, not `учить` + `ся`. It changes what the verb means.

## Glossing

11. Adjectives and pronouns follow the **Russian** gender: `большая
    книга` → "big(f) book".
12. There is no article in Russian - do not invent one in the gloss.
13. Where a single word does not exist, hyphenate.

## Phrases

14. Polite formulas, grammatical constructions, fixed terms:
    `добрый день`, `как дела`, `пожалуйста`, `у меня есть`,
    `надо было`, `дело в том, что`.
15. The base form of a phrase is the citation form, lower case.

## Spelling

16. **Stress marks are not part of the key.** `замо́к` and `за́мок` are
    written the same and keyed the same; the description tells them
    apart. `ё` is written as `ё` where the text writes it.
