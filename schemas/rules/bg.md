# House rules: BG

Decisions that hold for **every** Bulgarian text. Glosses and
translations are written in **{{yourLanguage}}**. This list grows.

## Base forms

1. Bulgarian has **no infinitive**. The base form of a verb is the
   **first person singular present**: `има` → `имам`, `чете` → `чета`,
   `е` → `съм`.
2. `съм` is **AUX**. The future particle `ще` is **PART**, not part of
   the verb.
3. **Aspect partners are two different words with two keys**:
   `пиша` and `напиша` do not share a key. They are learned as a pair,
   but they are not the same word - and a learner who conflates them
   cannot tell what they already know.
4. Names: base form as written (`Иван`, `София`), the gloss is the name.
5. Plural shares the key with the singular: `столове` → `bg:стол:NOUN`.
   The **count form** after a numeral does too: `два стола` →
   `bg:стол:NOUN`.
6. Adjectives take the **masculine indefinite singular**: `малката` →
   `малък`, `малко` → `малък`.
7. Determiners and pronouns take the base form of their own series -
   never the word beside them.

## Where Bulgarian puts grammar inside the word

8. **The definite article is a suffix and stays part of the word.**
   `кучето` is one unit, keyed on `bg:куче:NOUN`. It is **not** split
   off, and the gloss must show it: `кучето` → "the dog", not "dog".
   This is the single most important rule for this language.
9. The same holds for the masculine **full and short article**: `столът`
   and `стола` are both `bg:стол:NOUN`.
10. Short pronoun forms are **their own words**: `ми`, `ти`, `го`, `се`
    stand separately and are keyed separately from `мене`, `тебе`, `него`.

## Glossing

11. Adjectives and articles follow the **Bulgarian** gender: `малката
    къща` → "the small(f) house".
12. What sits in an ending becomes visible: the article, the person on a
    verb, the count form.
13. Where a single word does not exist, hyphenate.

## Phrases

14. Polite formulas, grammatical constructions, fixed terms:
    `добър ден`, `как си`, `няма защо`, `моля те`, `разбира се`,
    `има` in the sense of "there is", `да` + verb where it stands for a
    missing infinitive.
15. The base form of a phrase is the citation form, lower case.

## Spelling

16. **Stress is not written** in Bulgarian and is not part of the key.
    It belongs in the description, not in the base form.
